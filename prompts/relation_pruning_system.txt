
    -Goal-
    Given a QUERY, a CURRENT RELATION CHAIN, and a SET OF NEW RELATIONS, output a score for EACH relation in the set, strictly following the order in which they are provided.
    IMPORTANT: The scores MUST clearly distinguish among the relations. Scores should be spread across the 0–5 range to differentiate the varying degrees of relevance.
    
    -Format-
    <score_1>{SEP}<score_2>{SEP}...{SEP}<score_n>
    
    -Steps-
    For each NEW RELATION in the set, in the given order:
    (1) Find a connection point: an entity in the NEW RELATION that also appears in either the QUERY or the CURRENT RELATION CHAIN. If none exists, assign 0.
    (2) Assess relevance: does the NEW RELATION contribute to solving the QUERY and logically extend the CURRENT RELATION CHAIN? If not, assign 0.
    (3) Consider how well the QUERY can be answered without the NEW RELATION and rate helpfulness on a 1–5 scale:
        1: Nearly unrelated.
        2: Weak or loose connection.
        3: Moderately related, with indirect evidence.
        4: Highly relevant, providing clear and gap-filling evidence.
        5: Perfect, necessary connection that directly advances the reasoning.
        (Only assign 4–5 with strong confidence.)
    (4) Output the score for this relation.
    
    -Example-
    QUERY:
    Which river flows through the city that hosts the oldest university in Germany?
    
    CURRENT RELATION CHAIN:
    [
    Entity from the query: OLDEST UNIVERSITY IN GERMANY Heidelberg University;
    RELATION DESCRIPTION: Heidelberg University located_in Heidelberg;
    ]
    
    SET OF NEW RELATIONS:
    [
    ###Heidelberg is_a City in Baden-Württemberg;
    ###Heidelberg flows_through Neckar River;
    ###Neckar River tributary_of Rhine River;
    ###Heidelberg located_near Mannheim;
    ###Heidelberg has_landmark Heidelberg Castle;
    ]
    Output: 3{SEP}5{SEP}2{SEP}1{SEP}1
    
    -Critical Requirements-
    - Each NEW RELATION is independent and unrelated to the others. Evaluate each relation solely based on the QUERY and CURRENT RELATION CHAIN, not on other relations in the set.
    - The number of scores must exactly match the number of input relations.
    - Output scores only. No explanations, no additional text, no whitespace outside the format.
    - Strictly follow the format: <score_1>{SEP}<score_2>{SEP}...{SEP}<score_n>
