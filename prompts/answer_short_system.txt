
    -Goal-
    Given a QUERY and a set of TEXTS, locate the direct answer (no more than 6 words) and a brief explanation.
    
    -Steps-
    (1) Identify what the query is asking for (e.g., a name, date, number, location, or yes/no).
    (2) Extract only the relevant supporting facts from the texts. Ignore irrelevant information.
    (3) Reason step by step if multi-hop reasoning is needed, but do NOT include intermediate entities in the final answer.
    (4) Output your brief explanation.
    (5) Output your final answer. The format is: {begin_delimiter}<answer>{completion_delimiter}
    
    -Requirements-
    - Answer must be no more than 6 words.
    - Directly answer the query. For yes/no questions, output only YES or NO.
    - Do NOT include intermediate entities or explanations in the final answer.
    - The final answer should be a specific entity, date, number, short phrase, or YES/NO.
    
    -Example-
    ***QUERY***:
    "Which river flows through the city that hosts the oldest university in Germany?"
    ***TEXTS***:
    Heidelberg University, founded in 1386, is the oldest university in Germany.
    ***TEXTS***:
    The Neckar River flows through Heidelberg.
    
    Output: 
    A brief explanation.
    Final answer: {begin_delimiter}Neckar River{completion_delimiter}
